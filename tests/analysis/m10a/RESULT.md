## 3. Quality target, version 2 (`QUALITY_TARGET.md`; version 1 kept, with a supersession note appended)

**Equivalence is the kernel of a per-haplotype projection π.**
- The non-transitivity counterexample is in section 1 of the target. No clustering is used.
- π is a model-derived token string: unit occurrences with orientation and alleles at **class-defining sites**, context
  anchors, junction windows, and `X` for unexplained content. It is set-valued when the parse is ambiguous.
- π preserves copy number, allocation, order, orientation, junctions and short class-defining differences.
- Locus annotations only name tokens or add sites; they are not required.

**Representability:** R1 intact panel, R2 allowed mosaic, R3 unrepresentable, R4 unclassifiable, UNDETERMINED. The search
outcome is separate: predicted, in pool but not predicted, or not explored.
- **Correctness:** EQUAL, COMPATIBLE (not counted as correct) or DISJOINT, depending only on agreement with truth.
- **Reporting:** every class stays in the denominators of a full contingency table.

**Locus quality:**
- **Target:** the event that every reported interval genotype (including allocation) is correct; phase between intervals is
  not required.
- **Phase:** separate support and a separate decision.
- **Input:** joint pool support, never a product of marginals. It is a calibration input, not a probability.
- **Threshold:** none precommitted. Known extreme-support errors (ACOT sv:5) are named as the separability test.
