# Production quality target: proposal (specification only)

**Status: proposal, for Davide's approval.** Nothing here is implemented, calibrated or thresholded. No tolerance value is
approved and no PASS threshold is fitted. Section 7 lists every choice that needs approval.

**Priority (Davide).** Get the SV structure right. Exact panel identity and every SNP or indel matter less.

## 1. The target: the structural diplotype

**Structure of one haplotype** over a structural interval: the ordered list of segments `(unit class, orientation)`, with
the junction class between consecutive segments.
- **Unit class:** a member of a declared, model-bound structural alphabet. Examples: a C4 module with HERV long or short,
  C4A or C4B; CYP2D6, CYP2D7, or a hybrid with its switch region; an ACOT gene copy.
- **Orientation:** + or −.
- **Junction class:** which unit boundary or breakpoint region joins the two segments, located within a declared window.
- **Copy number** is the length of the list; **order** is the list order.

**Structural diplotype:** the **unordered** pair `{S(h1), S(h2)}` of the two haplotype structures.

**Equivalence.** Two diplotypes are equivalent iff they are equal as unordered pairs. Two haplotype structures are equal iff
all of the following hold:
- the same number of segments;
- the same classes, in the same order, with the same orientations;
- the same junction classes;
- every aligned segment pair is within the **declared small-difference tolerance**. Below it, SNVs and indels are ignored;
  an indel at or above the SV length is never ignored.

**What follows from this definition:**

| requirement | how the definition meets it |
|---|---|
| synonymous labellings identical | structures are computed from **sequence**, never from labels, so any labellings that spell sequences within tolerance project to one structure (as the compound target already does for exact bytes) |
| small differences tolerated | the declared tolerance absorbs them; they are reported as sequence diagnostics only |
| meaningful differences kept | copy number, homologue allocation, order, orientation and junction class are each part of the structure, so a difference in any of them makes two structures unequal |
| C4 **3+1 ≠ 2+2** | `{3 modules on one homologue, 1 on the other}` and `{2, 2}` are different unordered pairs of per-homologue structures, although the total dosage (4) is equal |
| homologue exchange | invariant by construction (unordered pair) |

**Truth-free.** The projection uses only the model, the panel-derived alphabet and the tolerance. It is computed for every
explored candidate, and never for truth, at run time.

**Relation to M9-J.** This is contract v2 with the exact-byte projection replaced by the structural projection. Interning,
the decision set, missing-projection handling, bounds, competitor status and resolution (including the M9-J.1
`INCOMPLETE_DECISION_SET` rule) carry over unchanged.

## 2. Genotype and phase are separate quantities

- **Genotype** (the target above) is the structural diplotype **within each structural interval**, including within-interval
  allocation. 3+1 against 2+2 inside one RCCX array is therefore a genotype difference, not phase.
- **Phase** is the cis/trans relation **between** structural intervals, and between a structure and nearby small variants.
  For example: which homologue carries the CYP2D6 hybrid **and** a given downstream duplication.
- The two are assessed with separate masses and separate decisions. Phase uncertainty never downgrades the genotype
  decision, and vice versa.
- A homozygous or single-interval locus reports phase as `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## 3. Reporting unit

**Primary unit:** one row per sample per **structural interval**. An interval is a declared, model-bound consecutive block
span, generated by a fixed rule from the panel. It is the same artifact type as `compound_interval_declarations_v1`, with
the structural alphabet attached.

**Locus summary:** a locus row that is confident only if every interval is confident, plus one phase row per adjacent
interval pair.
- **Recommended:** loci with one structural interval (most targets, including C4 RCCX) then have exactly one genotype
  decision.
- **Approval needed:** whether a locus with several intervals should also report one locus-level decision (section 7,
  item 5).

## 4. The quality decision (truth-free, at run time)

**Structural support.** Over the explored pool, contract-v2 bounds with the structural projection:
- `structure_support_lower`: the mass of groups whose every recorded labelling projects to the predicted structure;
- `structure_error_upper`: everything else, including missing projections.

**Decision:** `structure_quality` ∈ {`CONFIDENT`, `NOT_CONFIDENT`, `NOT_ASSESSED`}. It is `CONFIDENT` only if all of the
following hold:
1. the prediction is complete (`PREDICTION`, not `PROVISIONAL`; no budget or limit stop);
2. structural resolution is `RESOLVED`: every decision-set group is projected, onto one structure;
3. `structure_support_lower ≥ τ`.

`NOT_CONFIDENT` carries exactly **one** reason code, chosen by fixed precedence: `SEARCH_INCOMPLETE`,
`DECISION_SET_INCOMPLETE`, `DECISION_SET_STRUCTURES_DIFFER`, then `ALTERNATIVE_STRUCTURE_MASS`.

**τ is not chosen here.** It is set only by held-out calibration that Davide has approved (section 6). Until then, the
decision is not computed, and the support is reported as **uncalibrated**.

**Phase decision**, separately: `phase_quality` ∈ {`CONFIDENT`, `NOT_CONFIDENT`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`} per interval pair. Its
support is the mass of the predicted cis configuration, on the same pool. It has its own threshold, also uncalibrated.

**The prediction is always kept.** The decision annotates the prediction and never removes, replaces or masks it. This
covers the representative sequence and FASTA, under the unchanged policy.

## 5. Proposed production interface

| output | content |
|---|---|
| `<prefix>.structure.tsv` (new; one row per interval plus a locus row) | the predicted structural diplotype, as a readable string (for example `C4A-L·C4B-S / C4A-L`), copy number per homologue, `structure_quality`, one reason code, and the support (labelled uncalibrated until calibration) |
| `<prefix>.phase.tsv` (new) | one row per interval pair: predicted cis configuration, `phase_quality`, and its support |
| existing sequence outputs | unchanged: the representative sequence prediction and FASTA, now described as "one representative sequence of the predicted structure" |
| diagnostics (unchanged and kept) | `sequence_blocks`, `sequence_alternatives`, `sequence_compound`, dosage audit, rounds, ties, and every bound and count |

Users read one prediction, one quality decision and one phase row. Everything else stays in the detail files.

## 6. Evaluation against truth (offline only; no truth at run time)

**Per held-out sample and interval:**
1. Project each truth haplotype with **the same** structural projection, alphabet and tolerance.
2. Classify the outcome:
   - `STRUCTURE_CORRECT`: the predicted diplotype is equivalent to truth;
   - `DOSAGE_CORRECT_ALLOCATION_WRONG`: the multiset union of segments is equal, but the per-homologue split differs (for
     example 3+1 against 2+2);
   - `STRUCTURE_WRONG`: anything else.
3. **Representability.** Classify truth as `REPRESENTABLE` if some haplotype spellable from the **active panel** (after
   LOO or LZO curation) is structurally equivalent to it. Otherwise it is `UNAVAILABLE_IN_PANEL`: a missing class,
   orientation, junction or arrangement. This uses truth only to label the evaluation, never to make a candidate. A nearest
   representable structure may be computed as a diagnostic oracle only.

**Primary metrics,** each stratified by representability, locus and coverage:
- **Confident-call error rate:** the fraction of `CONFIDENT` calls not `STRUCTURE_CORRECT`. Confident calls on
  `UNAVAILABLE_IN_PANEL` truth **count as errors**: a correct caller should be `NOT_CONFIDENT` there.
- **Callability:** the fraction of `CONFIDENT` calls among representable cases.
- **Prediction accuracy regardless of the decision**, and the allocation-error share.
- **Phase:** confident-phase error rate and phase callability, over heterozygous interval pairs where truth phase is
  defined.
- **Sequence-level diagnostics** (not part of the target): edit distance and QV of the representative sequence against
  truth, and the count of tolerated small differences.

**Calibration protocol** (when approved):
- held-out families chosen and frozen beforehand, never seen in development;
- τ chosen on a calibration split for an **approved** target confident-error rate;
- the rate reported once on a separate test split, with an interval.

## 7. Choices that need Davide's approval

**Before implementation:**

| # | choice | why it matters | recommendation (not a value) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structural alphabet** per locus: which units, hybrids and HERV states are classes; generated by a fixed rule from the panel and reviewed per locus | defines what counts as structure at all | panel-derived, model-bound declaration (like the compound artifact), reviewed once per locus |
| 2 | **Small-difference tolerance:** the SNV / indel budget per segment, and the SV length at or above which an indel is never tolerated | decides which sequence differences are ignored | define it per segment; the preset's existing `min_variant_bp` (50) is an obvious **candidate** for the SV length, **not** approved |
| 3 | **Junction window:** how far a breakpoint may move and remain the same junction class, especially hybrid switch regions | CYP2D6/2D7 hybrid classes depend on it | windows in unit coordinates, declared with the alphabet |
| 4 | **Allocation inside the genotype** (3+1 ≠ 2+2) | short reads weakly identify allocation (C4 sv:7); including it lowers callability | include it in the genotype; report the dosage-only outcome as a diagnostic, not as a second decision |
| 5 | **Reporting unit** for multi-interval loci: per interval only, or also one locus decision | interface simplicity against information | per interval plus a locus row confident only if all intervals are |

**Before held-out calibration:**

| # | choice |
|---|---|
| 6 | the acceptable confident-call error rate (which determines τ), and the split and families design (LOO vs LZO, coverages) |
| 7 | the headline treatment of `UNAVAILABLE_IN_PANEL` truth (recommended: included, with confident calls counted as errors) |
| 8 | whether the phase decision is calibrated in the same campaign or later |

## 8. Out of scope for this proposal

No model, prior, filter, FASTA-policy, search or default change. No calibration, clustering, long-read work or new
families. The existing exact-sequence compound annotation stays as a stricter diagnostic.

---
**Superseded (appended by M10-A; the text above is unchanged as the historical record).** Version 2 is
`tests/analysis/m10a/QUALITY_TARGET.md`. It corrects three defects of this version:
1. pairwise "within tolerance" equality is not transitive. Version 2 defines equivalence as equality of a per-haplotype
   projection, with a class-defining position set in place of a tolerance;
2. representability is split into intact panel, allowed mosaic, unrepresentable, unclassifiable and undetermined, with the
   search outcome reported separately. Correctness depends only on agreement with truth;
3. the locus decision is about the joint event that every interval genotype is correct. It uses joint pool support as a
   calibration input, never a product of marginals, and precommits to no single support threshold.
